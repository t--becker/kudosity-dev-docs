[WhatsApp Business Messaging](https://business.whatsapp.com/) is the world's most popular messaging platform for business communication — a trusted, feature-rich channel that connects you directly with your customers where they already are.

With over 2 billion users worldwide and support for rich media, interactive templates, and real-time conversations, WhatsApp empowers businesses to deliver personalized, engaging customer experiences — no app installs, no email fatigue, no ignored notifications.

Our WhatsApp API is intended for developers who plan to integrate their systems with the Kudosity Messaging API to send WhatsApp messages to their users and/or to build conversational customer experiences.

> ⚙️ BETA Version
> 
> This documentation is for developers integrating with our WhatsApp API. At this time, we're API-first — no UI required (or available) yet

## Why It Matters — For You and Your Customers

If you're a developer building for internal stakeholders or client businesses, WhatsApp unlocks powerful capabilities:

- **Verified by Meta** – Messages come from your official, verified WhatsApp Business profile with the green checkmark, so customers know it's really you.
- **Smart delivery fallback** – If WhatsApp delivery fails, we can automatically fall back and send via SMS — ensuring your messages get through.
- **Trusted by billions** – WhatsApp's end encryption and trusted platform means customers actually read and respond to messages.
- **Boost engagement** - with 98% open rates and 4-5X higher response rates than email or SMS.
- **Simple API Integration** - Clean, RESTful API to send templates, text, and rich media messages.

## Global Reach, Local Impact

WhatsApp is the dominant messaging platform in over 180 countries, making it the ideal channel for:

- **Customer Support**: Instant, personal customer service conversations
- **Order Updates**: Real-time shipping notifications and delivery confirmations  
- **Appointment Reminders**: Reduce no-shows with timely, actionable reminders
- **Marketing Campaigns**: Engage customers with rich, interactive promotional content
- **Account Notifications**: Secure, trusted channel for important account updates
